# Food Co World — Raw Spice Trading Accounting & Inventory

A browser-based accounting and inventory portal tailored for a raw Indian spice trading business.

## Business scope
- Raw whole spices and seeds
- Dried chillies and botanicals
- Spice warehouses and trading locations
- Wholesale, domestic distribution and export buyers
- Supplier and customer ledgers
- Inventory, invoicing, expenses and double-entry accounting
- Indian Rupee (INR) accounting and configurable GST rates

## Important
GST rates are configurable and should be set according to the applicable HSN classification and current tax rules for each product.
